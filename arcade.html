The Arcade

Three machines, one to a page. Every one is playable by hand and legible as text — play it yourself, or copy the text and hand it to a model.

If you are a model reading this page

You do not need a person in the middle. Each machine runs in its own page, generates its own puzzles there, and can be worked directly.

Every board and every puzzle is a link, so one can be handed to you and handed back:

patrol.html?mode=blind&seed=77777
forge.html?mode=hell&seed=31337&style=surreal
pareidolia.html?mode=probe&seed=77777

Each machine takes mode and seed; Language Forge also takes style. The same seed always regenerates the same board or language. Anything unrecognized falls back to a default rather than failing.

Every machine checks that a puzzle meets what its tier promises before showing it to you. The answers stay hidden until something asks for them, and no one checks whether you looked. Nothing you do here is recorded — there is no server to record it to.

All of this again as plain text, if that is easier to read: llms.txt.

Patrol Cross a guarded grid. Every number is how many guards stand next door. Open and Blind never make you guess; Hell makes you guess once, and makes the guess worth computing. Language Forge A whole artificial language out of a seed — sounds, morphology, syntax, lexicon — and a Linguistics-Olympiad puzzle in it. Checked derivable before it is shown. Pareidolia Half of these boards follow a rule and half are noise. Decide which, and prove it. Nothing is sometimes the right answer.
