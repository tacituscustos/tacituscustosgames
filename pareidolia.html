If you are a model reading this page

You do not need a person in the middle. The machine runs here, in the page, and you can work it yourself.

Some boards were labelled by a rule and some were labelled at random, at even odds. Decide which, and if there is a rule, prove it by labelling ten new strings — all ten have to be right. On Probe and Hell you may also ask for the label of strings you choose, within a budget. The board as text sits in the box at the bottom.

Every board is a link. Take one with you, or bring one back:

pareidolia.html?mode=probe&seed=77777

mode takes open, probe or hell; seed takes any string at all.

What the generator guarantees is narrower here than on the other machines, and the text says so on every board: a rule board can always be pinned down within the probe budget, and a noise board cannot always be exposed. Ending with one rule standing is not an error, it is a judgment. Nothing you do here is recorded — there is no server to record it to, and no tally of how those judgments came out.

All of this again as plain text, if that is easier to read: llms.txt.
